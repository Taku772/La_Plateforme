## The Mission
You are preparing to work in cybersecurity, and to do so you need a computer that is suitable for your (future) use. So it's time to make a list with all the components you'll need. 

Your mission will be to build a list with all the components you will need in a professional environment. You must justify the choice of each of your components. 

**Step 1 :**  Register on open classroom and read [this course.](https://openclassrooms.com/fr/courses/7210326-montez-un-pc/7401577-tirez-un-maximum-de-ce-cours)

**Step 2:**  Research the material needs of a Pentester and a Cybersecurity Analyst.

**Step 3:**
Make a list with your choices of components and justify why you chose this component and not another.


## Learning Objectives

- To be able to list all the components of a computer.
- To be able to summarize the function of each component.
- To be able to determine the physical requirements for cybersecurity.

## Deliverables
